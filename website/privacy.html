Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CruizX handles personal data in the CruizX mobile app and on the CruizX website.

1. Data We Collect

Depending on how you use CruizX, we may process:

  • Account data such as email address, display name, user ID, and optional profile avatar.
  • Location and navigation data used for map view, routing, ETA, speed display, and road-limit related features.
  • Convoy data such as convoy membership, shared pins, chat messages, and live member positions (where enabled).
  • Community alerts data you submit or upvote, including category, location, timestamp, and optional text.
  • Parent Mode data (if enabled), including linked accounts, shared location/speed settings, and related alerts.
  • Device and app data needed for security, stability, and ad delivery, including ad identifiers when ads are shown.

2. Why We Use Data

  • To provide core app features such as navigation and convoy mode.
  • To display and moderate community alerts.
  • To authenticate users and protect accounts (including MFA).
  • To support subscriptions and ad-supported free use.
  • To improve reliability, prevent abuse, and maintain safety-related functionality.

3. Services and Processors

CruizX currently integrates services such as:

  • Supabase (authentication and backend data storage/realtime).
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob (ads in free tier).
  • Firebase (only when explicitly enabled in app configuration).
  • Apple/Google platform services for payments and app distribution.

4. Ads and Tracking

CruizX may show ads to free users. On iOS, tracking permission is requested via App Tracking Transparency when applicable. Ad services may process device-level identifiers according to their own privacy terms.

5. Storage and Retention

Some preferences and session-related values are stored locally on your device (for example language and user settings). Backend records are retained as required to provide app features, enforce security, and meet legal obligations.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared with infrastructure and service providers that process information on our behalf, and where required by law.

7. Your Choices

  • You can manage app permissions (location, tracking) in OS settings.
  • You can choose whether to use optional features like Parent Mode.
  • You can stop using the app and request account-related support.

8. Children

CruizX includes Parent Mode functionality. Parents or guardians are responsible for supervising use and linking behavior where applicable.

9. International Transfers

Data may be processed in countries where our service providers operate. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the services in use.

10. Changes

We may update this policy over time. Material updates are reflected by the date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, use the support/contact channels available via CruizX and cruizx.se.
