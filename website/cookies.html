Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy describes how CruizX uses cookies and similar technologies on the website and related local storage mechanisms.

1. What We Use

  • Website storage (for example local storage) to remember language and basic UI preferences.
  • Essential technical storage to keep pages working correctly across sessions.
  • Third-party technology that may set or access identifiers when ad or platform services are used.

2. Why We Use It

  • To provide core website functionality and a stable experience.
  • To remember your selected language and similar preferences.
  • To support services connected to app distribution, subscriptions, and ads where relevant.

3. App-Related Device Storage

In the mobile app, local device storage may be used for account state, settings, and feature preferences. This helps the app function correctly, including language, vehicle settings, and user experience options.

4. Managing Cookies and Local Storage

  • You can clear cookies/local storage in your browser settings at any time.
  • You can control app permissions and tracking settings in iOS/Android system settings.
  • Blocking certain storage features may reduce website or app functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

Where CruizX relies on third-party providers (for example ad and backend services), their technologies and policies may also apply.

6. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy over time. Material updates are shown by the date at the top of this page.

7. Contact

For cookie-related questions, contact us through CruizX support channels and cruizx.se.
